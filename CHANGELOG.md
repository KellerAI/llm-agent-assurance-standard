## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **Breaking (major, per `CONTRIBUTING.md:56`; release number chosen at release time, maintainer decision D8).** The LAAS policy `conformance/laas/laas.rego` (bundle `laas-fin-1.1.1`, `conformance/laas/data.json:3-4`) now matches the normative prose (maintainer rulings SD-1 to SD-3, bead laas-yju):
  - SD-1: an undetermined tier fails closed to CT4. `expected_ct` is 0 only when `action.effect_surface.external_effect` is boolean `false` (`laas.rego:34`). Absent, null, or non-boolean values yield CT4 (`laas.rego:30`), and the lattice branch requires `true` (`laas.rego:39`). Per `standard/LAAS.md:43-44`. Records that omitted `effect_surface` previously evaluated as CT0.
  - SD-2: at CT4 (`human_approval_floor_ct`) a model-lineage verifier no longer satisfies independence. `LAAS-OBL-IND-001` fires for a passed `verifier.type == "model"` on a non-blocked action (`laas.rego:225`). Per `standard/LAAS.md:88-89`. Deterministic or human verifiers remain acceptable; the HUM-001 approval is the human in addition.
  - SD-3: `LAAS-OBL-RES-001` also requires evidence referenced in the trace where a residual tolerance applies (CT2 and above) on non-blocked actions. A numeric `residual_error_bound` without a non-empty `evidence_refs` array of non-empty strings fires (`laas.rego:248`), and a Bucket-B action (anything other than a passed deterministic verifier) without a bound fires (`laas.rego:255`). Per `standard/LAAS.md:103`. New top-level input field: `evidence_refs`.
  - Migration: emitters must set `external_effect` explicitly and emit `evidence_refs`. `scripts/laas/emitter.py` does not yet emit `evidence_refs` (follow-up).
- Standards renderings restyled as KellerAI publications (patch, documentation only; maintainer ruling 2026-10-01, raised by Sachin Murthy). The four renderings in `docs/laas/standards/` and their PDF covers and themes are now published by KellerAI and formatted after the IEEE, ISO/IEC, NIST, and Federal Reserve SR-letter drafting conventions; none is presented as issued, numbered, addressed, or copyrighted by those bodies. Each rendering opens with the publisher line, the attribution line, and the designation `KellerAI LAAS 1.1 · <FORMAT> rendering · Draft 1 (2026)` (`laas-ieee.md:1-10`, `laas-iso.md:1-12`, `laas-nist.md:1-5`, `laas-nist.md:13-15`, `laas-sr.md:1-10`). Each cover carries the attribution line (`pdf/covers/ieee-cover.html:11`, `pdf/covers/iso-cover.html:11`, `pdf/covers/nist-cover.html:11`, `pdf/covers/sr-cover.html:9`), and each theme's running header and footer carry the KellerAI designation and an unofficial-publication notice (`pdf/themes/ieee.css:8`, `:20`; `pdf/themes/nist.css:8`, `:22`; `pdf/themes/iso.css:8`, `:14`; `pdf/themes/sr.css:8`, `:16`). Removed: the SR cover's agency letterhead and addressee block; the SR rendering's distribution, contact, and signature block, replaced by a Questions line (`laas-sr.md:188`); the body-style designations; and the ISO/IEC copyright lines from the ISO cover and the ISO theme footer, the cover line replaced by `© 2026 KellerAI contributors. Licensed under Apache-2.0.` (`docs/laas/standards/pdf/covers/iso-cover.html:9`) and the footer by an unofficial-publication notice (`docs/laas/standards/pdf/themes/iso.css:14`). All four renderings and covers now carry the KellerAI copyright line (`laas-nist.md:11`, `pdf/covers/ieee-cover.html:11`, `pdf/covers/nist-cover.html:11`, `pdf/covers/iso-cover.html:9`, `pdf/covers/sr-cover.html:9`). The same terms are used in `docs/laas/standards/pdf/README.md`, the `pdf/build.sh:2-8` comments, `README.md:33-35`, `AGENTS.md:17` and `:57`, `docs/articles/index.md:8-9`, and the GitHub Pages link labels (`.github/workflows/pages.yml:40-43`).

### Added

- 25 tests in `conformance/laas/laas_test.rego` for SD-1 to SD-3 (suite 44/44; `opa test conformance/` 115/115); 6 of them pin the rules' guards (`blocked`, verifier-passed, `evidence_refs` type checks, and tolerance scope). The `_base_ct4` fixture gains `evidence_refs` (`laas_test.rego:67`).
- Documentation is updated to the new behaviour and bundle id: `conformance/laas/README.md`, `docs/agents/enforcement.md` and `glossary.md`, the `docs/laas` emitter, backtest, and osi-adapter docs, the IEEE, ISO, NIST, and SR renderings, and the profiles.

- Controlled-language profiles for LAAS decision traces under `docs/laas/profiles/` (minor, additive): a shared rule base (`ste-core.md`) defining twelve core writing rules, a cross-domain hedge ban list, four language-conformance levels (`LC-0`–`LC-3`), a recommended gate policy (advisory at CT0–CT1, warning at CT2, blocking at CT3–CT4), and a mapping showing how controlled language strengthens each of the twelve existing obligations; plus five industry profiles — `banking.md`, `healthcare.md`, `seo-adsense.md`, `real-estate.md`, `contracting.md` (building, HVAC, solar) — each with a domain rule set, an approved noun and verb dictionary, a forbidden-terms table, STE-compliant decision-trace field templates, and a worked example. Three profiles state a more restrictive deviation for a defect class whose harm does not scale with the tier, and so is blocking at every tier including CT0: dose and abbreviation notation (healthcare), occupant-description language (real estate), and unit notation (contracting). Machine-readable dictionaries derived from each profile are at `docs/laas/profiles/glossary/*.json`. The profiles are informative and adapt ASD-STE100 principles: they define no new obligation, tier, or threshold, and `standard/LAAS.md`, `conformance/laas/data.json`, and `conformance/laas/laas.rego` are unchanged.
- Four additional controlled-language profiles for LAAS decision traces under `docs/laas/profiles/` (minor, additive): `legal.md` (legal and contracts, `LAAS-STE-LEGAL-DRAFT-1.0`), `insurance.md` (claims and policy administration, `LAAS-STE-INS-DRAFT-1.0`), `software-docs.md` (runbooks, API docs, incident comms, `LAAS-STE-SWD-DRAFT-1.0`), and `education.md` (syllabi, assignment briefs, student-facing instructions, `LAAS-STE-EDU-DRAFT-1.0`) — each with a domain rule set, an approved noun and verb dictionary, a forbidden-terms table, STE-compliant decision-trace field templates, and a worked example. One profile states a more restrictive deviation for a defect class whose harm does not scale with the tier, and so is blocking at every tier including CT0: identifier notation, version notation, and credential redaction (software-docs). Machine-readable dictionaries derived from each profile are at `docs/laas/profiles/glossary/{legal,insurance,software-docs,education}.json`. The profiles are informative and adapt ASD-STE100 principles: they define no new obligation, tier, or threshold, and `standard/LAAS.md`, `conformance/laas/data.json`, and `conformance/laas/laas.rego` are unchanged.
- Change-surface veto for the trust-dial bot-PR auto-merge policy (`conformance/trust_dial.rego` + `trust_dial_data.json`): a base `auto-merge` verdict now requires every changed file to be within an allowlist (`.github/workflows/**`, `.github/dependabot.yml`) and outside a frozen deny-list (`conformance/**`, `docs/**`, `standard/**`, `scripts/**`, root governance docs); otherwise the verdict is vetoed to `block`. Missing `changed_files` input fail-safes to `hold-for-review`. New `changed_files` input is supplied by `.github/workflows/trust-dial-gate.yml`.
- Guarded codeowner auto-merge: PRs authored by a codeowner (new `codeowner_actors` list in `conformance/trust_dial_data.json`) receive a base `auto-merge` verdict from `conformance/trust_dial.rego` subject to the same change-surface veto, fail-safe (`hold-for-review` on missing `changed_files`), and per-cycle merge budget as Dependabot PRs. `.github/workflows/trust-dial-gate.yml` now also runs for codeowner-authored PRs (Dependabot metadata step skipped) and supplies the new `pr_author` OPA input.
- Advisory annex `docs/laas/j-space-internal-state-annex.md`: maps externally verified J-space / global-workspace interpretability findings (Gurnee et al., Anthropic — Transformer Circuits, 2026) to LAAS internal-state assurance concepts; access-consciousness-scoped (no sentience or phenomenal-consciousness claim), explicitly non-conformant/advisory per the sibling j-space-research repo's ADR-001, and conjecture-flagged for forward-looking theses (TH-004–TH-006) while treating C5–C8 and TH-002/TH-003 as supported.
